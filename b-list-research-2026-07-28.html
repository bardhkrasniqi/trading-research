Standing rules for this regime

1) Size by ATR — risk a fixed account fraction per ATR-based stop, never fixed shares. 2) RVOL is the confirmation filter — structure breaks without 15m RVOL > 2.5 revert more often than they run. 3) VWAP is the intraday bias line — longs above, shorts below, no exceptions in extreme-ATR names. 4) Flat into binaries — no day-trade inventory through FOMC 14:00 or through an AMC print in the reporting name. 5) The morning re-anchor — these levels are the map; the daily premarket scan (gap, slot-RVOL, PMH/PML) re-anchors entries each session. 6) Oversold ≠ long — in LH/LL structure, oversold RSI is fuel for the next leg down until a higher-low prints on the 1H or daily.
