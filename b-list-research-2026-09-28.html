Methodology and data notes

Pipeline, rubric, data caveats and sources

Pipeline: TradingView Desktop (CDP) → dedicated "B List Research" layout → up to 500 bars per timeframe per symbol (W / D / 240 / 60 / 15, regular sessions) → indicators computed locally: EMA 10/21/50/200, RSI-14 (Wilder), MACD 12/26/9, ATR-14 (Wilder), 20-bar relative volume, fractal (2-2) swing pivots, 252-day high/low distances, relative strength vs SPY as return differentials. Trend matrix rubric per cell, ±1 for each of six inputs: close vs EMA21, close vs EMA50, EMA21/EMA50 stack, EMA50 five-bar slope, RSI zone (≥55 = +1, ≤45 = −1, else 0) and MACD histogram sign — score ≥+4 Up, ≤−4 Down, else Chop; cells lacking an EMA21 or EMA50 return "—".

Data caveats. Captured Monday Sep 21 2026 at 08:11 ET, before the open; no errors were reported across the 22 symbols and 500 bars loaded on every timeframe for every symbol with sufficient listing history. Twenty names ended on Friday Sep 18's completed session on every frame. QQQ and META carried a one-tick pre-market bar dated Sep 21 as the last bar of their daily and weekly frames (open, high, low and close identical); both bars were removed from the stored bar history and those four frames were recomputed with the collector's own routine, and relative strength against SPY was recomputed, so that all 22 names describe the same data cut. The raw scan with the stubs is kept separately in the archive and is used nowhere. The weekly column is the completed Sep 14–18 bar; its badges, histograms and extensions are final and are quoted as such.
